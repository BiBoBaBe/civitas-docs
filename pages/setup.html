Civitas is in private testing with a few people from the Marinara community. This page says what you would be running, what setup involves, and what would help most.

What you are trying

A working prototype: the Civitas service, the Marinara bridge, and Tremont as the playable world. Tremont has a cast of about forty with routines, relationships, rumours, plans, a phone, and a World screen you can inspect while playing. The other settings on this site are sketches, not beta-ready worlds.

What you need

  • Marinara with the Civitas bridge extension installed.
  • The Civitas service, which runs separately on Bun 1.4 or later, with a world attached to your chat.
  • A text model connection for narration and for the scene-reading pass. The reader needs a model that can follow a schema; a model that cannot should have the pass turned off rather than left to guess.
  • Optionally, image generation, which needs its own configured backend and a graphics card. Without one, there are no pictures and nothing else changes.

There is no one-click installer yet. Expect some hands-on setup, and expect to ask. Model calls and any GPU use cost whatever your providers charge; there is no fixed per-turn price to promise, though a turn's brief is a few hundred tokens and the scene reader is a second, smaller call.

How to get in

Reply to the Discord post where this link was shared. Say what you are running Marinara on and the sort of roleplay you enjoy, and we can work out the setup from there. This site does not collect sign-ups.

What would help most

  • Continuity. Did a promise, a payment or a change in a relationship get recorded properly? Did it still hold three sessions later?
  • Knowledge. Did somebody know something they could not have heard?
  • Playing. Was it obvious what you could do and where to find things on the World screen?
  • Feel. Did the world give you interesting openings, or get in the way?
  • Practical. Slow turns, confusing setup, phone layouts, broken pictures.

For a bug, the useful parts are what you expected, what happened, and the steps that got you there. A screenshot or a short excerpt helps. Keep API keys and private conversations out of reports.
