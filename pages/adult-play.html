Civitas supports adult play, for adults, and it is off until you turn it on. This page describes the mechanics in the same register as the rest of the site. There is nothing explicit on it or anywhere else here.

Two switches, both off

Adult material is opt-in per conversation and lives in the narrator's voice contract. Adult pictures are a second, independent switch, so you can play an adult story with entirely safe-for-work illustrations. Both are off in the example world by default.

Everybody in every world is an adult. That is not a setting. The image pipeline refuses anything that reads otherwise, at every layer, whoever wrote the prompt.

Consent is an object

Agreement is a record rather than a tone. Terms are clauses two people negotiate and sign, frozen once in force. The engine refuses a narrator that tries to reword what somebody put their name to. A safeword ends everything the instant it is spoken, costs the person using it nothing, and is never a breach; the narrator is handed the word in full every turn. Underneath it, either party can end any agreement at any moment, for any reason, at no cost.

Where a third person is involved, an invitation is three separate yeses and any one no ends it. Your own yes is never something a narrator can write for you.

Desire is earned, and it is slow

Feelings towards you rise at a capped rate per day of world time, on each axis independently, and nothing caps how fast they fall. The numbers are on People and feelings. Things somebody keeps are gated on their trust in you, one a day, face to face, in order. A narrator that tries to hand over something unearned is refused with a note rather than an error.

Drink, and the morning after

Somebody two drinks into an evening says things they would not say sober, and a world that refuses them the words is lying about the scene. So the world tracks whether somebody's guard is down, on a short clock, and lowers the trust gate by a small amount, capped at 0.3 in total with everything stacked. Drunk is the next item or two coming within reach, not a skeleton key. When something comes out early, the belief records that it did and why, which is what makes the morning after possible.

What is off, and what they changed into

The world keeps appearance as state: what has come off, what is showing, and what somebody changed into. What came off belongs to the flat it happened in and ends when you leave. What they changed into travels with them and ends when the day turns over. This is why the prose and the pictures agree three beats later.

Privacy is physical

A shut door means the person fifteen feet away did not see what happened. It does not mean they are unaware: they know two people went in and locked it, because that happened in front of them. In an open room, something said quietly is between two people. Everybody else gets a roll, and what they can win is that she told him something she does not tell people, with no subject attached. See Places and doors.

Anonymity

An app handle is not a second character. It is the same person, with all their feelings, shifts and rumours attached. Whether you know who they are and whether they know who you are are two separate facts. Recognising somebody takes a tell, planted when the handle was created, and your own message. A reveal creates something you both hold in confidence, which is then an ordinary secret that can get out by the ordinary rules.

Pictures fail closed

With the adult picture switch off, only outer layers can come off and only a short list can be shown. With it on, the rule is unchanged: an image contains exactly the people it is meant to contain, every frame is checked after it is drawn, and a frame that fails three times produces no picture. Where a two-person arrangement has not been measured to a standard worth shipping, the system draws something else that is correct, or nothing, and leaves the previous picture on screen.

Why this is on the same site

Nothing on this page is an adult feature with adult machinery behind it. Terms are the treaty system. The trust gate is the secret system. Appearance state is what keeps somebody in their work clothes behind a bar. Doors work the same way for everybody. An engine that knows who was in the room, what they were told, and what they agreed to is useful for a conspiracy, a raid party's rules, a treaty, and for this.
